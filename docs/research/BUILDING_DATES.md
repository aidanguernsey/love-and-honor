# Miami University (Oxford) building dates — research notes

Compiled 2026-09-30 for `data/timeline.json` (Spike B, step 3c). **Nothing here has been checked against University
Archives records; every entry is `verified: false`.** Please treat dates as leads, not facts, until verified.

## Summary

- **177 buildings and landmarks**: 137 standing, 26 demolished, 4 absorbed into other buildings, 1 moved away, 9 with unknown fate.
- Confidence: **44 corroborated** (2+ sources agree), **100 single-source**, **13 conflicting**, 5 decade/circa estimates, 15 with no date found.
- 127 entries are linked to a present-day OpenStreetMap footprint (including 4 buildings demolished in 2026 that OSM still shows).
- Scope: buildings and landmarks owned or used by Miami (including Western College and Oxford College buildings). Fraternity houses and private homes near campus are out of scope.

## Method

1. Roster of current buildings: named buildings inside Miami's campus outline in OpenStreetMap (129), plus Miami buildings just outside it.
2. Dates: Miami's own Historical Timeline (five era pages, the backbone), Wikipedia articles in *Category:Miami University buildings and structures* (44), the Smith Library's *Walking Tour of Oxford's University Historic District* (2008/2014), Miami news releases (2013–2024), The Miami Student, and local news for 2025–26 demolitions.
3. Where sources disagree, the entry uses Miami's timeline year by default, or another source's year when there is a stated reason (given in the notes); all alternatives are recorded.
