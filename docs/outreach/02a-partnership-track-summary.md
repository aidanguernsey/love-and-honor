# Love & Honor — the Myaamia relationship in the game (draft for review)

*Attachment to draft 2. It is written for the Myaamia Center, so it describes the plan in plain terms. It
reflects GAME_DESIGN.md §20. Nothing on this page has been reviewed by the Center or the Tribe.*

**About the game.** Love & Honor is a free, non-commercial fan game about Miami University. The player guides the
university from its 1809 charter to the present day: placing buildings on a map of the real Oxford terrain and
making decisions about academics, students and the community. It is a personal hobby and portfolio project. It
is not affiliated with or endorsed by Miami University.

**How the relationship appears**

- **It is a partnership track, not a resource.** The player can make long-term, recurring commitments. Examples:
  annual support for the Myaamia Center's language and cultural research, support for Myaamia students (for
  example a heritage scholarship, if one exists as described), and respectful naming and signage on campus.
- **It grows slowly and has no fail state.** Nothing about it is a one-time purchase or a "grind". It is meant to
  mirror how real relationships are built over time.
- **Its effects are framed as institutional integrity.** In game terms it affects the university's Heritage,
  students' sense of belonging, and academic quality in related departments. It never awards "points for
  representation".
- **History section (in-game encyclopedia):** short factual entries on how the university came to carry the Miami
  name, the formal relationship with the Miami Tribe of Oklahoma, the Myaamia Center, and the athletic name change
  made at the Tribe's request. Each entry is sourced, and the wording is taken from university- or Tribe-published
  material.
- **A later story chapter** would cover the relationship and the name change from the university's perspective.
  It would use only text the Tribe has approved; without that approval, the chapter would be left out or reduced
  to published facts.

**What the game will never include**

- Myaamia imagery, regalia or symbols, or any mascot based on Native people.
- Invented words, names, stories or ceremonies.
- Stereotyped characters or content of any kind.
- Myaamia-language words, unless the Center or Tribe provides and approves them.

**Questions for the reviewer**

1. Is the partnership framing appropriate? Is there anything you would change or remove?
2. Which terminology should the game use (for example "Myaamia", "Miami Tribe of Oklahoma", "Miami people")?
3. Are there sources you'd recommend the history entries rely on?
4. Would you like to see the exact in-game text before release? We will not release it without that review or a
   decision to use published wording only.
